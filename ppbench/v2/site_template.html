What every system was asked to build

One core-set object with a reference assembly, cited real-world claims and annotated joints. Each system received the prompt and the in-the-wild photo; tool-using models also received the 57-part mixed pool (Tier A) or the pool plus part creation (Tier B).

Scoreboard

Graded scores in [0, 1], mean over seeds (± spread). The mark gives the worst status across seeds. Tiers are never averaged together. Click a row to open its designs and every raw measurement.

Designs

Seed 0 of every system, rendered from the condition image's camera and three standard views. Parts are coloured individually.

Claim coverage

Every sentence of the task's attribute and functional prompts is backed by a claim. Each cell is the rule-based score for that claim (mean over seeds): required parts P by role coverage, attributes A by computed proxies, subsystems F as presence · placement · connection, kinematics K by joint matching.

How each number is computed

Rule-based first. Every design is voxelised once (4 mm) and every dimension reads that grid, the declared roles, materials, joints and sequence, the task's claims, the reference, and a population of 117 other Artiverse swivel chairs.
