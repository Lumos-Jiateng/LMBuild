What review rounds change

The same system and tier with 1, 2 and 3 rounds. A round ends with submit; before the next one the model sees a check of its design and four renders. Lines connect the mean score per round for four dimensions; thumbnails are seed 0.

Claim coverage

The rule-based score per claim, mean over seeds, at the selected round count. Required parts P by role coverage, attributes A by computed proxies, subsystems F as the mean of presence, placement and connection, kinematics K by joint matching.

Rounds

How each number is computed

Rule-based first, on the 3D structure. Every design is voxelised once (4 mm); the rule-based dimensions read that grid, the declared roles, materials, joints and sequence, the task's claims (P, A, F, K), the reference chair, and 117 other Artiverse swivel chairs. No rendered image enters a rule-based score. Where a rule cannot decide what a structure means (does this part look like a lever, does this joint realise a slide), VLM columns judge evidence rendered from the same 3D design and must cite labelled parts.

Evaluator specification, metric by metric

Each card is derived from the evaluator's code (file:line references) and says exactly what is read, how every sub-metric is computed, how they combine, when a status is set, which constants are used, what the golden tests cover and what is known to be imperfect.
