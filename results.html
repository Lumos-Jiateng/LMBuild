Results

Main setting: object name plus in-the-wild photo, Tier B (catalogue plus part creation), first review round, seed 0. Domain-specific generators run in their native single-output setting. Scores are ×100 and averaged over the 200 objects; a missing design scores 0. N/A marks metrics a system's output cannot support.

Bold marks the best score per column among the agents (closed APIs and open models). Several generator scores are high by construction (for example, brick generators cannot produce colliding parts); see how to read single metrics.

Stability

Any balanced 50 objects give the same ranking

We split the 200 objects into two disjoint 50-object samples with the same mix of harness conditions (13 + 37 and 12 + 38 objects with and without a catalogue contact sheet) and repeated the draw 1,000 times. Without running anything new, system scores on the two halves agree closely on every metric.
