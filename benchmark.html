The benchmark

No single dataset covers the range of structures LMBuild targets, so we combine LEGO models, part-segmented shapes, engineering CAD assemblies, articulated everyday objects and complete open-source hardware products. Every object comes with a reference assembly and with parts, attributes, subsystems and kinematics grounded in cited Wikipedia and Wikidata evidence.
